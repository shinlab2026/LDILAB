About the Lab

LDI Lab aims to create learning processes and outcomes that are meaningful to learners, educators, and educational practice.

Grounded in learning theory and instructional design, we use AI and multimodal data, including brain signals, discourse, behavior, interaction, and digital learning traces, to understand how learning unfolds and to design better learning environments, instructor support, adaptive feedback, and AI agents.

At LDI Lab, intelligence is the capacity to sense, understand, and support learning through theory, data, and AI.

Current research projects

Project 01

Human Learning and Agency in AI-Mediated Environments

Learning Strategies for Cognitive Offloading and Self-Regulation

We investigate how learners can maintain and strengthen human agency in AI-mediated learning environments. Focusing on cognitive offloading, metacognition, self-regulated learning, and decision-making, we examine how the use of AI reshapes human learning processes. We design learning strategies and support tools that enable learners to use AI productively while sustaining critical thinking, responsibility, and control over their own learning.

hub

Project 02

HyFlex Learning Models and Support Tools

Analyzing Brain and Behavioral Synchrony to Foster Presence

We investigate how social and cognitive presence emerge in HyFlex environments where in-person and remote learners participate together. Using fNIRS and behavioral data, we examine patterns of synchrony among learners and instructors and use the findings to develop HyFlex learning models and support tools that foster presence and engagement.

forum

Project 03

Automated Discourse Analytics for CSCL

A Human-AI Framework and Explainable AI (XAI)-Based Analysis

We use AI to analyze learner discourse generated in PBL and CPS settings and to understand collaborative problem-solving and knowledge-construction processes. We develop a Human-AI Framework that combines researchers’ pedagogical judgment with AI-assisted analysis and apply explainable AI to enhance the interpretability and educational usability of analytic results.
