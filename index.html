Project 01

Human Learning & Agency in AI-Mediated Environments

FOCUS

Human-AI Interaction Multimodal Learning Analytics Adaptive AI Support

APPROACH

Human-AI Interaction Physiological, behavioral, and discourse data AI learning agent design and development

We examine how AI shapes self-regulation, decision-making, and agency, and design AI support that sustains critical thinking and learner control.

Multimodal Analytics and Support Design for HyFlex Learning

HyFlex learning Social & cognitive presence Learner engagement

Explainable Discourse Analytics for Collaborative Learning

FOCUS
